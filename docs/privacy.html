Privacy Policy for Fishbowl Game

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Fishbowl Game ("the app") is a local, offline party game. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Summary

Fishbowl Game does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information the App Stores

The app uses local device storage (via Android's standard app storage mechanism) to save:

  • Player names you enter
  • Team names and scores
  • Words/phrases submitted for gameplay

This information is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us, to any server, or to any third party. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Information We Do Not Collect

The app does not:

  • Connect to the internet or any external server
  • Use analytics, tracking, or advertising services
  • Access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos
  • Share any data with third parties
  • Require you to create an account or provide personal identifying information

Children's Privacy

Fishbowl Game does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since the app collects no data at all, it can be used safely by players of any age under adult supervision as appropriate for the game's content.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if the app adds online features), an updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
