Fishbowl Game

Free · Offline · No ads

A fun, team-based party game where players guess words or phrases from a "fishbowl" across three rounds. No paper, no pens, no setup hassle, just open the app and play.

Fishbowl is a local, offline game. Everything runs on the device, no accounts, no internet connection, no ads.

How the Game Works

Teams take turns trying to guess as many words as possible within a time limit, using the same set of words across three rounds:

ROUND 1

Taboo (Verbal Explanation), Describe the word using any words except the word itself.

ROUND 2

Charades Act out the word without speaking.

ROUND 3

One Word Describe the term using only one word.

Getting Started

  1. Set the number of teams, players, words per player, and the time limit.
  2. Add player names, the app randomly assigns everyone to teams.
  3. Each player submits their words for the fishbowl.
  4. Play through all three rounds, tapping Correct as words are guessed.
  5. See the final scores on the results screen.
